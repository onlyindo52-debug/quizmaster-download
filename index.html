// what it does

Simple on purpose.

QuizMaster does a few things and tries to do them well. Here's the short list.

Write it, publish it, done

Admins can create quizzes, add questions and publish them when they're ready. No spreadsheets or paper handouts.

Results right away

Finish a quiz, see how you did. No waiting.

See your progress

Old results stick around, so you can see if you're improving.

Lives on your phone

It's an Android app, so a quick practice round fits in the gap between lectures or the ride home.

// the story

Two students, one idea, a lot of late nights.

QuizMaster started as a college project. We wanted to build something people would actually use, not just something that gets a grade and then sits in a folder.

A quiz app felt right: it has real users on both sides (the person writing the questions and the person answering them), and every small detail matters when someone is in the middle of a test.

// faq

Questions you might have.

Why is Android warning me about the install?

Android shows that prompt for any app that doesn't come from an app store. You're installing the APK directly from this page, so it asks you to confirm once. You can turn the permission back off afterwards.

Do I need an account?

Yes. You sign in with the account you've been registered with, then you'll see the quizzes available to you.

Is it on the Play Store?

Not right now. For the moment the APK on this page is the way to get it.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Tell Priyanshu or Riya. It's a student project, so feedback genuinely helps us make it better.
